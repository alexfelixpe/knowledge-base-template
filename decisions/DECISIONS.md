# <PRODUCT> decision log

Running log of decisions the team has aligned on. Each entry: date, what was
decided, who was in the decision, and the source. Same subject and same
substance: amend the entry and say what changed. A different ruling: a new
entry, and the old one gains "superseded by the YYYY-MM-DD entry". Old text is
never rewritten or deleted.

An entry needs a date, the decision, who was in it, and a source. No source
means it is not ready for the log. It is an open item in `decisions/open/`.

Entry format, newest first inside each section:

```
- **YYYY-MM-DD: <what was decided, as one bold line>**
  <Who was in the decision, and one to three lines of reasoning.>
  Source: <link, or meeting name and time>.
```
